Adidas Sponsors Both Finalists in a $2.8 Billion World Cup

Spain and Argentina meet today at MetLife Stadium in the most commercially valuable FIFA World Cup ever sold — and both finalists wear the same three stripes.

Last 5 World Cup Finals
The brand story is bigger than the scoreline. Spain (Euro 2024 champions) and Argentina (2022 world champions, 2024 Copa América winners) face off in the final at MetLife Stadium on 19 July 2026 — MetLife Stadium. Both national teams are outfitted by Adidas, which supplies 14 of the 48 competing teams — more than Nike (12) or Puma (11) — Footy Headlines. Whichever side lifts the trophy, Adidas wins.

This tournament closes a 72-year chapter: Germany switches to Nike from 2027, in a deal reported at roughly €100 million a year — about double Adidas' previous rate. Source: Sportico, nss sports. Even losing its founding federation, Adidas still outfits more 2026 World Cup teams than any other brand — including both finalists.
