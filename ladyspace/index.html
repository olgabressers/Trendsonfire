The Space

The transformation

Lady Space is where a woman becomes a lady. Six disciplines: business etiquette, carriage and movement, table etiquette, floristry, the ordering of a home and Chinese astrology.

Each discipline stands on its own, and each is learned to be used: at a table, in a room, in a meeting.

Lady Space was founded by Natalia Hendrix and Alena Bressers.

Programme

Six disciplines

01

Presence

Business etiquette

Introductions and seating, the business meal, written correspondence and dress.

Carriage and movement

Posture and the line of the back, walking in heels, sitting down and standing up.

02

The Table

Table etiquette

Cutlery, glassware and linen, the order of a setting, and the order in which a table is served.

Floristry

Composition, proportion and colour in flowers, for a table and for a room.

03

The World

Ba Zi

Chinese astrology. The four pillars of a birth: year, month, day and hour, and how a chart is read.

Feng shui

The basic ordering of a home: orientation, light, the placement of furniture and the flow of a room.
