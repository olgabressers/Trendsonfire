Influencer & Creator Commerce — Platform Selection

Purpose: help a brand or retailer identify the right platforms to find, recruit, manage and pay influencers and creators for their business, comparing commerce platforms, paid discovery/campaign-management tools and ambassador programs across the active market.

Scope: platforms for finding, recruiting and transacting with influencers and creators. Onboarding a new creator platform or campaign-management tool typically takes several weeks — a relevant constraint ahead of peak shopping periods such as Black Friday and the December holidays.

Where a brand lists product, a creator tags or links it, and the creator earns a commission set by the brand on each completed sale.

Which to Use, and When

Every commission figure below is brand-set, not platform-fixed — the ranges shown are what platforms report brands typically choose.

NeedPlatform typeStarting costTypical lead time to launch
Sell through existing large creator audiences fastCreator commerce platforms (LTK, ShopMy, TikTok Shop, Meta)Free to join — brand only pays commission on completed salesDays, once approved
Find and vet specific creators to approach directlyDiscovery & campaign-management SaaSFrom $69–199/mo (Heepsy, Modash) up to enterprise contractsWeeks — search, outreach, negotiation, content production
Turn existing customers into an always-on referral channelAffiliate & ambassador platformsFree to $39/mo plus a % of affiliate sales (Shopify Collabs, Refersion)Weeks to set up; compounds over time once live
Holiday-season timing: TikTok Shop's own commission-protection and payout terms run to 30–31 days per sale, and creator vetting on paid discovery platforms typically adds several more weeks before content goes live — a brand targeting Black Friday or December needs a decision on which platform(s) to use in place well before the peak weeks themselves.
