Interview Brief — Head of Operations · Leadership team

SkinVision: a Class IIa skin-cancer device with six operational areas to run as one

Founded in Amsterdam in 2012, SkinVision runs an artificial intelligence skin-cancer risk assessment certified as a Class IIa medical device, with more than 90% sensitivity and 89% specificity. Nearly 3 million people have used it, the image database holds more than 6 million skin images, and insurer partnerships can reach 20 million people. High-risk results are reviewed by dermatologists. Operations sits on that clinical pathway: support, clinical review, care access, billing, tooling and service analytics. The Head of Operations reports to the chief executive officer and owns all six.

Sources: SkinVision pricing page, SkinVision LinkedIn job description, SkinVision Head of Operations Candidate Briefing, skinvision.com, Halldale (28 January 2019) Updated 18 October 2026
Key conclusions
The company
Amsterdam-based, founded 2012. Class IIa medical device under European Union regulation. Nearly 3 million people helped, more than 6 million skin images, and a team of dermatologists who review high-risk cases.
The role
Head of Operations, reporting to the chief executive officer, member of the leadership team, Amsterdam (hybrid). Owns six areas: customer service, clinical review, care access and follow-up, billing and revenue operations, business systems, and service performance analytics.
The mandate
A growth role. Success at 12 months includes measurable specificity improvement at unchanged sensitivity, a stratified view of service performance, and at least one opportunity found by Operations taken forward.
Key opportunity
Six small teams already produce one dataset. Joined and stratified by device, skin type, market and age, it becomes the evidence Regulatory needs and the direction Product needs.

Role Analysis
3 structural challenges this role is built to solve
01
Six areas, one dataset
  • Support, clinical review, care access, billing, tooling and analytics run semi-independently, each small in headcount and wide in subject matter
  • Each team sees its own slice below the noise threshold; the pooled, stratified view is where real problems show
02
Specificity gains with a sensitivity floor
  • The algorithm is tuned for high sensitivity, so human review recovers specificity by reducing false positives
  • Sensitivity is a floor and is only reported alongside specificity held constant; any movement is an immediate escalation to Clinical and Regulatory
03
You cannot stratify what you do not collect
  • Device model, operating system, age band, market, language and self-reported skin type need consistent, joinable capture before any analysis
  • Smaller strata, such as darker skin types in a majority-European user base, are the hardest to measure; "we cannot yet tell" is reported as a status
