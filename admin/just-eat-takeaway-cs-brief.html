Interview Brief — Head of Customer Service NL · Customer Service EU

Just Eat Takeaway.com: 175 CS jobs cut, then this role opened

€5.09B group revenue (FY2024, continuing operations), 20,000 employees, delisted from Euronext in November 2025 after Prosus's take-private deal. In September 2025, ~175–200 Dutch customer service jobs were cut as AI absorbed first-line tasks at Enschede. The 100-person team named in this job ad is what's left after that cut — and under new owner Prosus, "returning Just Eat Takeaway.com to positive growth" is the named top priority for the year ahead.

Sources: Just Eat Takeaway.com Newsroom, Prosus, DutchNews, stockanalysis.com, job ad (provided) Updated 7 July 2026
Key conclusions
The company
Founded 2000 in Enschede as Thuisbezorgd.nl. Merged with Just Eat plc in 2020. Sold Grubhub in 2024 to refocus on Europe. Acquired by Prosus in 2025; delisted from Euronext Amsterdam 17 November 2025 — now a Prosus subsidiary, not a listed company.
The role
Head of Customer Service NL — lead a 100-person team (Enschede or Amsterdam, hybrid), report to Director of CS EU, own SLA and CSAT targets, own the CS P&L, run cross-border initiatives with Central CS, and act as project lead simplifying processes country-wide.
Programme context
September 2025: ~450 jobs cut group-wide, ~175–200 in the Netherlands, concentrated in CS and sales administration at Enschede, as AI assistants and restaurant self-service absorbed tasks. The 100-person team in this job ad is the post-restructuring team.
Key opportunity
Prosus named returning Just Eat Takeaway.com to positive growth its top FY2027 priority, with 25% order growth already reported in selected cities from new product and AI initiatives. The CS mandate is to run a smaller, AI-augmented operation at higher SLA and satisfaction — proof points from Richemont's own cost-centre-to-commercial-channel transformation map directly onto this brief.

Role Analysis
3 challenges this role is built to solve
01
AI restructuring already underway
  • ~450 jobs cut group-wide in Sept 2025, ~175–200 in the Netherlands, mainly customer service and sales administration at Enschede
  • Restaurants now self-manage menu changes; AI assistants handle first-line consumer issues; humans handle escalations and exceptions
  • The 100-person team named in this job ad is the team that survived that cut — the mandate is to run it leaner, not rebuild it
Source: DutchNews, Sept 2025 · RetailDetail
02
Return-to-growth mandate under a new owner
  • JET segment revenue down 6% in Prosus's first reported fiscal year (FY2026, to March 2026)
  • Prosus named "returning Just Eat Takeaway.com to positive growth by year-end" its top FY2027 priority for the business
  • 25% order growth already reported in selected cities from new product initiatives and the group's AI platform — proof points to build on, not from zero
Source: Investing.com, Prosus FY2026 slides
03
A two-sided, thin-margin NL market
  • Deliveroo exited the Netherlands at the end of 2022, unable to compete with Thuisbezorgd.nl and Uber Eats on cost and scale
  • Uber Eats remains the only serious rival to Thuisbezorgd's leading NL position
  • Globally, Uber Eats leads food delivery revenue, ahead of Delivery Hero, with Just Eat Takeaway.com behind both — defending NL share happens in a market where the parent group is not the global scale leader
Source: PYMNTS, Deliveroo NL exit 2022 · ElectroIQ / Statista, Uber Eats statistics
The AI platform behind the restructuring — Large Commerce Model (LCM): Prosus's proprietary AI infrastructure now powers operations across its ecosystem including Just Eat Takeaway.com — serving 118 million users and processing 15 million orders monthly across 40+ use cases. FY2027 guidance keeps adjusted EBITDA roughly flat because Prosus is reinvesting in JET's transformation rather than harvesting margin — this is framed internally as an investment year, not a cost-cutting year. Source: Investing.com, Prosus FY2026 slides, 29 Jun 2026

The gap this role has to close: the app itself is well rated (4.6/5, 725K ratings) — ordering works. Trustpilot and PissedConsumer, where people go specifically to complain, tell a different story: recurring themes are missing items, orders marked "delivered" but not received, slow email-only support with no phone option, and refund delays. High satisfaction with the product, low satisfaction with service recovery when something goes wrong — a classic CS-function gap, not a product gap.
Sources: Trustpilot — thuisbezorgd.nl · Apple App Store, NL · PissedConsumer — justeattakeaway.com. No CSAT/NPS figure is publicly disclosed by Just Eat Takeaway.com or Thuisbezorgd.nl.

Priority stack — 5-step approach
#ActionWhy firstOutput
1 Map the post-restructuring team — current SLA/CSAT baseline, escalation volumes by type, where AI containment is and isn't working Can't set realistic targets on a team that changed shape four months before this role started, without knowing where it actually stands today Baseline SLA/CSAT report; escalation-type breakdown; AI containment rate by issue type
2 Stabilise the escalation layer — the 100-person team's real job now is complex cases; make sure hand-off from AI to human is clean A messy AI-to-human hand-off is the most visible failure mode right after a restructuring — this is where CSAT gets won or lost first Clear escalation criteria and routing; hand-off SLA
3 Rebuild the P&L and cost-to-serve model for the smaller, more automated team Financial targets set for the old headcount don't apply to the new operating model — needs a rebuilt baseline before it can be managed Updated CS P&L and cost-per-contact model
4 Align with Central CS EU on the growth mandate — what "return to positive growth" means operationally for the Dutch CS function Prosus set this as a group priority; the NL CS plan needs to be built to serve it, not run in parallel to it Agreed NL contribution to the FY2027 growth priority
5 Embed continuous improvement — a test-and-learn cadence for workflow changes, with quarterly benchmark review against the pre-restructuring baseline Testing culture must be built into the new operating model from the start, not retrofitted after Monthly test-and-learn cycle; quarterly benchmark review
