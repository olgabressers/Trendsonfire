Interview Brief — Head of Customer Service NL · Customer Service EU

Just Eat Takeaway.com: Head of Customer Service, Netherlands

€5.09B group revenue (FY2024, continuing operations) and roughly 20,000 employees worldwide. The company delisted from Euronext Amsterdam in November 2025 following its acquisition by Prosus. In September 2025, the Dutch customer service organisation moved to a more AI-enabled operating model, with the team now concentrated on the escalations and judgement calls that AI cannot yet make. Under Prosus, returning Just Eat Takeaway.com to sustained growth is a named priority for the year ahead — and Dutch customer service is central to that ambition.

Sources: Just Eat Takeaway.com Newsroom, Prosus, DutchNews, stockanalysis.com, job ad (provided) Updated 7 July 2026
Key conclusions
The company
Founded 2000 in Enschede as Thuisbezorgd.nl. Merged with Just Eat plc in 2020. Sold Grubhub in 2024 to refocus on Europe. Acquired by Prosus in 2025; delisted from Euronext Amsterdam 17 November 2025 — now a Prosus subsidiary, not a listed company.
The role
Head of Customer Service NL — lead a 100-person team (Enschede or Amsterdam, hybrid), report to Director of CS EU, own SLA and CSAT targets, own the CS P&L, run cross-border initiatives with Central CS, and act as project lead simplifying processes country-wide.
Programme context
In September 2025, Just Eat Takeaway.com introduced AI-assisted first-line support and restaurant self-service tools in the Netherlands, evolving the CS operating model. As part of that transition (publicly reported at the time), the Dutch CS and sales administration teams moved to a new, more focused structure — today's 100-person team is concentrated on escalations, exceptions, and the judgement calls AI cannot yet make.
Key opportunity
Prosus named returning Just Eat Takeaway.com to positive growth its top FY2027 priority, with 25% order growth already reported in selected cities from new product and AI initiatives. The CS mandate is to run a leaner, AI-augmented operation at higher SLA and satisfaction — a transformation Olga has led before, turning Richemont's Client Relations Center into a commercial channel while hitting every SLA.
