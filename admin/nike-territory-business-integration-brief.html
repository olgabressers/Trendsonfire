Interview Brief — Lead, Territory Business Integration, Northern Europe · EMEA (Europe, Middle East and Africa) Business Integration

Nike EMEA: $12.6 billion in revenue, one seasonal Code process to run across every Territory

Nike, Inc. reported $46.4 billion in revenue for fiscal 2026, with EMEA (Europe, Middle East and Africa) contributing $12.6 billion. The company is running its Win Now actions and standing up a Sport Offense, with a new Vice President and General Manager for EMEA since 2 February 2026. The Lead, Territory Business Integration for Northern Europe sits in the EMEA Business Integration function at the Hilversum headquarters and represents it in the Territory Leadership team, working with the Territory General Manager on the Code process, the Rhythm of the Game and Territory marketplace activation.

Sources: Nike, Inc. fiscal 2026 and first-quarter fiscal 2027 results, Nike annual report on Form 10-K, Nike newsroom, SGB Executive, Puma, Nike job posting Updated 2 October 2026
Key conclusions
The company
Nike, Inc. — $46.4 billion fiscal 2026 revenue (flat reported, down 2% currency-neutral), $3.1 billion net income, approximately 73,000 employees and 988 Nike-owned stores. EMEA is one of four Nike Brand geographic segments and generated $12.6 billion, up 3% reported and down 3% currency-neutral.
The role
Lead, Territory Business Integration, Northern Europe — Hilversum, within EMEA Business Integration and the Territory Leadership team. Owns Territory alignment to the end-to-end Code process, the Territory Rhythm of the Game, translation of the consumer concept map into Territory activation plans, and contribution to the EMEA Business Integration community.
Company context
Chief Executive Officer Elliott Hill is running Win Now actions and standing up the Sport Offense, which organises teams by sport across Nike, Jordan and Converse. César Garcia became Vice President and General Manager of EMEA on 2 February 2026. First-quarter fiscal 2027 results, reported 1 October 2026, show total revenue of $11.2 billion, down 5% currency-neutral.
Key opportunity
Turn the global consumer concept map into one Territory activation plan that Nike Direct, Digital, Retail, Wholesale Partners and Key City all run from, on a single seasonal Code calendar with clear accountability to the Territory General Manager.

What the Sport Offense is
  • Structure — teams organised around sports across Nike, Jordan and Converse, instead of men’s, women’s and kids’ categories.
  • Priority sports — running, basketball, football, training and sportswear.
  • Win Now focus — five key cities (New York, Los Angeles, London, Beijing, Shanghai) and three countries (the United States, China, the United Kingdom).
  • First-quarter fiscal 2027 — training grew globally, led by EMEA, according to the earnings call transcript.
Sources: SGB Executive, 15 September 2025 · Nike first-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call transcript, 1 October 2026.

What Code means
  • Code — Nike’s end-to-end seasonal process, run through key moments, gates, milestones and deadlines.
  • Consumer Code, EMEA — the EMEA version of the process, led by the EMEA Consumer Code Director, which aligns Consumer and Marketplace within the geography.
  • Gates — the checkpoints inside Code that the Territory has to meet on schedule.
  • Territory role — owns Territory alignment to the process and leads the Territory seasonal Code forums, in partnership with the EMEA Code team.
Sources: LinkedIn job description · Natasha Blom, LinkedIn.

Nike organisation, 2025–2026

AreaStructure
GeographiesThe leaders of the four geographies — North America, EMEA, Greater China, Asia Pacific and Latin America — joined the senior leadership team and report to the Chief Executive Officer, Elliott Hill. César Garcia leads EMEA from 2 February 2026.

Sport OffenseTeams organised by sport across Nike, Jordan and Converse instead of men’s, women’s and kids’ categories.
Pace restructuring, 1 October 2026Four geographies become three — Americas, Asia Pacific and Greater China, and EMEA — in fiscal 2028. More resources move to countries, territories and cities, with local teams owning more of their markets. Savings target of about $2.5 billion cumulative through fiscal 2031, implementation charges of roughly $1 billion pre-tax, $300 million of severance recognised in fiscal 2026. Job-cut decisions begin in calendar 2027. A new Nike campus in India is planned.
