A nulurencontract is an employment contract, not a way around one. Loonheffing, employer charges and sick pay all apply — only the hours vary. It is the instrument supermarkets use for exactly this kind of rostered team. Three conditions: call-up at least four days ahead or the shift is paid anyway; the wage obligation can be excluded only for the first six months; and after twelve months a fixed-hours contract must be offered based on the previous year’s average — which applies immediately to staff already here for years.

Call-up hours do not fit management. A nulurencontract is legal for anyone, but the higher WW premium makes it dearer than a fixed contract, and the twelve-month rule would convert a full-time manager to fixed hours within a year regardless. Robin states the hours as Römer 35, Wisman 40, Linders 35 and Jasperse 30 — 140 a week between four. That is structural full-time work, and the twelve-month rule would convert it to a fixed contract immediately. These are the hardest positions to defend: a contractor working full time for one client cannot have a second one. Putting the whole management layer onto payroll costs €58–68K a year.

The payments in scope are real, summed from the annual accounts above. The 25–40% is an assumption and not a quoted rate — the actual assessment depends on gross-up and on credit for income tax the contractors have already paid. What is established: enforcement of schijnzelfstandigheid resumed on 1 January 2025; an assessment cannot normally reach further back than that unless opzet is found, in which case it runs five years. Verzuimboetes are held off through 2026 by cabinet commitment; vergrijpboetes became possible on 1 January 2026.

Three exposures sit on top of the tax, and none of them are in the table. Article 7:610a BW presumes an employment contract after three months of weekly work, with the burden on the company to disprove it — forty people can claim back pay, 8% vakantiegeld, pension and transitievergoeding, and that is civil, so the 1 January 2025 cut-off does not apply. At the €15–20 an hour described for the Adecco switch the rate sits below the 2026 statutory minimum of €14.71 once holiday allowance is added, which brings in the Nederlandse Arbeidsinspectie as a second regulator. And article 36 Invorderingswet reaches anyone who directed the company als ware hij bestuurder, whether or not they appear in the Handelsregister.

The fix is the cheapest thing on this page. One year of doing it properly costs less than the smallest possible assessment, and it stops the clock — every further month on the current arrangement adds to a bill that is already running. Doing it before an inspection rather than after also removes the opzet argument, which is the difference between the €297–476K line and the €793K–€1.27m one.
