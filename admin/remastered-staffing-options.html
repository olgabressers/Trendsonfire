The nulurencontract is the instrument supermarkets use for exactly this team. Three conditions: call-up at least four days ahead or the shift is paid anyway; the wage obligation can be excluded only for the first six months; and after twelve months a fixed-hours contract must be offered based on the previous year’s average — which applies immediately to staff already here for years.
