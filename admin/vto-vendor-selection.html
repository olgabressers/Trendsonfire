Virtual Try-On — Vendor Selection

Purpose: help an emerging brand or marketplace select a virtual try-on vendor for their own site, comparing published technology, integration method, target client base, trial terms and pricing across the active market.

Scope: visual try-on vendors only — direct API/SDK integrations and Shopify App Store apps a brand can run on its own site, plus platform-hosted options shown for market completeness. Non-visual size-recommendation technology (True Fit, Bold Metrics, Fit Analytics and others) is a separate category, covered at trendsonfire.com/technology/fashion-sizing-tech.html.

Lowest cost to start: TryPoint's pay-as-you-go tier has no monthly fee at all ($0.29/try) — the only vendor with a genuinely zero-commitment entry point. For a guaranteed free monthly allowance, bitStudio's Shopify-only free plan covers up to 1,500 tries/month (50/day) at $0 — the largest explicitly published free allowance of any vendor.
Lowest cost at scale (12,000+ tries/month): A&A Virtual Try-On's Premium plan ($249.99/mo, 3,750 tries, $0.06/try overage) costs $746.19 at 12,020 tries/month — cheaper than FASHN.AI and Kivisense ($888.00 each at that volume). Calculation: $249.99 + (12,020 − 3,750) × $0.06 = $746.19.
