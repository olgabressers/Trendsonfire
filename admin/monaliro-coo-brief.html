Company & Role Brief — Chief Operating Officer

Monaliro: €8,000–€12,000/month, application window closed 15 September

Monaliro B.V. is an Amsterdam-based technology company (∼50 employees) building content-protection, content-screening and fan-engagement infrastructure. Its flagship product, F2F.com, is a creator subscription platform in the adult content space, generating 2.46M monthly visits with 79% of traffic from the Netherlands and Belgium. The Chief Operating Officer role was posted via recruiter TwoBirds Company, paying €8,000–€12,000 gross per month, with a founder-led scale-up mandate to build out leadership structure ahead of planned acquisitions.

Sources: LinkedIn job posting, monaliro.com, f2f.com, theorg.com, KVK Chamber of Commerce register, Semrush, Trustpilot, twobirdscompany.com Updated 22 September 2026
Critical: the job posting's stated application deadline was 15 September 2026 — seven days before this brief was built. Applications required a CV emailed directly to monaliro@twobirdscompany.com; the posting states LinkedIn's own "Easy Apply" is not accepted as a valid application route.
Key conclusions
The company
Monaliro B.V. (KVK 84060050), Amsterdam, founded 2021 under parent monaliro holding B.V. (KVK 84057521). Builds compliance and fan-engagement infrastructure; its own platform F2F.com is described in the job posting as having grown "from challenger to market leader in the Netherlands and Belgium within years."
The owners
F2F.com was co-founded roughly four years ago by three people: Jean-Micheal Veen van der Zwaan (current CEO), Frederik van den Bosch, and Martijn van Lom. LinkedIn currently lists Frederik van den Bosch's title as Chief Operating Officer — the same seat this job posting is recruiting for.
The role
Full-time, permanent, Amsterdam, €8,000–€12,000 gross/month depending on experience. Mandate: build a leadership team, improve connectivity between leadership and staff, clarify accountability, and prepare the company for growth and future acquisitions.
Competitive context
F2F.com competes in the global creator-subscription category dominated by OnlyFans ($7.85B gross revenue, FY2025). Semrush's own comparison tool names Fancentro.com, not OnlyFans, as F2F.com's closest traffic-matched competitor.

Company
Monaliro & F2F.com
What monaliro builds
A Platform-as-a-Service company: APIs and infrastructure for content protection, content screening and fan-engagement, built to satisfy the compliance requirements of payment platforms. Stated mission: "to harness technology for a safer, open, and transparent internet."
What F2F.com is
Friends2Follow (F2F.com), monaliro's own flagship product, built on that infrastructure: a subscription-based platform where creators sell exclusive content directly to fans, operating in the adult content category. Described internally as a "fast-growing tech scale-up," now in its third year.
Registration
monaliro B.V. — KVK 84060050, Keizersgracht, Amsterdam. Parent: monaliro holding B.V. — KVK 84057521, Nieuwe Herengracht 123A, Amsterdam, registered as director since incorporation on 28 September 2021.

Role Analysis
What the Chief Operating Officer is being hired to do
Core mandate
Build a leadership team where disagreement is voiced and decisions collectively owned; improve connectivity between leadership, team leads and staff; clarify responsibilities and accountability; develop leadership and organisational structure; prepare the company for growth and future acquisitions, including integrating new businesses.
Required background
Extensive experience in founder-led scale-ups; organisational development, hiring and talent development; a track record of improving structure and decision-making without adding bureaucracy; technology affinity; experience with compliance and reputation issues. M&A and post-merger integration experience preferred.
Candidate profile named
The posting asks for someone "solid without ego" who can "connect while remaining authentic," stays composed under pressure, and is comfortable working in the adult industry sector.
Recruitment process & terms
RecruiterTwoBirds Company, Amsterdam-Duivendrecht (∼13 employees). Positions itself as placing "professional COOs" alongside founders — "a new kind of entrepreneurship" where the founder keeps strategy and the COO runs operations.
ProcessFour steps per TwoBirds' own site: introduction call, a half-day "Quickscan" to define the ideal COO profile, meetings with three shortlisted candidates within 10 business days, then onboarding.
Application routeCV by email to monaliro@twobirdscompany.com. LinkedIn's own Easy Apply is explicitly stated as not accepted.
Deadline15 September 2026 — already passed.
Start dateAs soon as possible, per the posting.
ContractFull-time, permanent, Amsterdam. Seniority level listed as Director.
Sources: LinkedIn job posting, twobirdscompany.com/recruitment-support.
