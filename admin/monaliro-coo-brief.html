Company & Role Brief — Chief Operating Officer

Monaliro: 50 people built F2F.com into the Netherlands and Belgium's market leader

Monaliro B.V. is an Amsterdam-based technology company (∼50 employees) building content-protection, content-screening and fan-engagement infrastructure. Its flagship product, F2F.com, is a creator subscription platform in the adult content category, generating 2.46M monthly visits with 79% of traffic from the Netherlands and Belgium, and described by its own recruiter as having grown "from challenger to market leader" in both countries within years. The Chief Operating Officer role, posted via recruiter TwoBirds Company, joins a four-person leadership team (Chief Executive Officer, Chief Strategy Officer, Chief Commercial Officer, Chief Financial Officer) with a mandate to build formal leadership structure ahead of planned acquisitions.

Sources: LinkedIn job posting, monaliro.com, f2f.com, theorg.com, KVK Chamber of Commerce register, Semrush, Trustpilot, twobirdscompany.com Updated 22 September 2026
Key conclusions
The company
Monaliro B.V. (KVK 84060050), Amsterdam, founded 2021 under parent monaliro holding B.V. (KVK 84057521). Builds compliance and fan-engagement infrastructure; its own platform F2F.com is described in the job posting as having grown "from challenger to market leader in the Netherlands and Belgium within years."
The owners
F2F.com was co-founded roughly four years ago by three people: Jean-Micheal Veen van der Zwaan (Chief Executive Officer), Frederik van den Bosch (Chief Strategy Officer) and Martijn van Lom (Chief Commercial Officer). Joel holds the fourth leadership seat as Chief Financial Officer. This role joins them as the fifth.
The role
Full-time, permanent, Amsterdam, Director seniority level. Mandate: build a leadership team, improve connectivity between leadership and staff, clarify accountability, and prepare the company for growth and future acquisitions.
Competitive context
F2F.com competes in the global creator-subscription category dominated by OnlyFans ($7.85B gross revenue, FY2025). Semrush's own comparison tool names Fancentro.com, not OnlyFans, as F2F.com's closest traffic-matched competitor.

Company
Monaliro & F2F.com
Product description
Monaliro presents itself as a Platform-as-a-Service (software infrastructure sold to other businesses) company: APIs for content protection, content screening and fan-engagement, built to meet payment platforms' compliance requirements. Its only named product is F2F.com (Friends2Follow), a subscription platform where creators sell exclusive photos, videos and direct messages to fans behind a paywall, plus tipping and pay-per-view content — the same mechanics OnlyFans popularised. Monaliro's own site does not name any external business customers for its underlying infrastructure beyond F2F.com itself.
Core product features
Creator subscriptions, direct messaging, tipping and pay-per-view content, run on infrastructure Monaliro describes as built for "high-availability" and AI-assisted content screening. Stated mission: "to harness technology for a safer, open, and transparent internet."
Payment & tax ecosystem
F2F.com's own Creator Awards sponsor list names Paxum, a payment processor serving the adult-content industry specifically, and TaxMate, a creator tax-filing service — the specialist payment infrastructure this category typically needs rather than mainstream processors.
Registration
monaliro B.V. — KVK 84060050, Keizersgracht, Amsterdam. Parent: monaliro holding B.V. — KVK 84057521, Nieuwe Herengracht 123A, Amsterdam, registered as director since incorporation on 28 September 2021.
Sources: monaliro.com, f2f.com, f2fawards.com sponsor list.

Role Analysis
What the Chief Operating Officer is being hired to fix
Operational Challenges
3 structural challenges this role is built to solve
01
No formal leadership structure yet, at ∼50 people
  • The posting's own core mandate: build a team "where differences are voiced and decisions collectively owned," and improve connectivity between leadership, team leads and staff
  • The leadership team is four people — Chief Executive Officer, Chief Strategy Officer, Chief Commercial Officer and Chief Financial Officer — and this role joins as the fifth seat
  • Required background is explicit: "improve structure and decision-making without adding bureaucracy"
Source: LinkedIn job posting
02
Operating in a payment category card networks actively police
  • In 2021, Mastercard's rules forced OnlyFans to announce, then reverse, a full ban on the adult content central to its business — the category's clearest precedent for payment-processor risk
  • Visa's own dispute-monitoring programme flags a merchant as "Excessive" at a 1.50% dispute ratio (tightened from 2.2%, effective April 2026); Mastercard's Excessive Chargeback Programme starts at 1.5%
  • F2F.com already routes payments through an adult-industry-specialist processor (Paxum) rather than a mainstream one, per its own sponsor list
Source: CNN, 2021, Visa VAMP 2026 thresholds
03
A stated acquisition mandate, with no deal yet announced
  • The posting names preparing the company "for growth and future acquisitions, including integrating new businesses" as a core deliverable
  • No acquisition by Monaliro or F2F.com has been publicly announced as of this brief
  • Even experienced acquirers take 12–18 months to fully integrate a deal, per McKinsey/PwC research across 200+ transactions — the posting is recruiting the person who would build that capability from zero
Source: LinkedIn job posting, McKinsey
