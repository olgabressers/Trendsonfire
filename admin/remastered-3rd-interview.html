Fixed cost base — why volume + B2B win
  • 89% of the cost base is fixed — every visitor past break-even adds €19.63 straight to profit. Volume and B2B drive the result, not cost-cutting.

Unit economics — three lines to own

  • €1.83M of cost is fixed — it does not move whether 80K or 125K come through the door. So volume and B2B drive the result, not cost-cutting.
  • Every visitor past break-even adds ~€19.63 straight to profit — filling the room is the fastest lever there is.
  • Capacity-constrained, not market-constrained — €7.3M ceiling at one site. Growth = fill it, add B2B, then a second site.

Your ask — if terms come up

  • 4-day week at their full-time number (~€125K) — effective ~€154K/yr, 5th day free. Framed as pay for the mandate, not pro-rata.
  • Growth bonus + milestones — 10% of revenue above €2.3M, plus cash at €3M / €4M / €5M. Upside you trigger yourself.
  • Equity only as phantom equity with a guaranteed put — never plain shares that pay only on a sale.
