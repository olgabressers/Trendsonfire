What actually moves this business

89% of the cost base is fixed and you sit only 3.7% above the 93,388 break-even — extreme operating leverage. Every visitor past break-even is €19.63 straight to profit. And you're at 29% of a 339,450 ceiling — so the job is demand + yield, not capacity.

The CAC test — the number that wins the room

Reaching 125,000 means buying 28,000 more visits. Each is worth €19.63 in contribution — so marginal CAC must stay under €19.63, or growth loses money. My commitment: 112,000 visitors profitably, not 125,000 at any cost. The 28K comes from free levers first (conversion, yield, off-peak fill), repeat rate (founder-dependent), then B2B — not 28,000 bought clicks.
