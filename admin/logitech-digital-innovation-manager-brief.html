Terms used on this page
DTC — Direct-to-Consumer: sales made through a brand's own website rather than a retailer.
eTail — online sales made through a third-party retailer's own website (Amazon, Best Buy) rather than the brand's own store.
UGC — User-Generated Content: reviews, photos and videos shoppers create themselves.
R&R — Ratings & Reviews: the star-rating and written-review system on a product page.
Guided selling — an interactive tool that asks a shopper a few questions and recommends the matching product.
Configurator — a tool that lets a shopper build or customise a product before buying it.
Omnichannel — coordinating the shopping experience across a brand's own site, physical retail and third-party retailers as one consistent journey.
AOV — Average Order Value: the average amount spent per completed order.
ROBO — Research Online, Buy Offline: a shopper researches a product online before buying it in a physical store.
FTE — Full-Time Equivalent: a standardised way of counting headcount.
KPI — Key Performance Indicator: a metric used to track performance against a target.
CAGR — Compound Annual Growth Rate: the average yearly growth rate over a multi-year period.
Industry Context
Top 5 digital innovation trends in electronics retail
The five shifts this role's roadmap sits inside
1. Generative AI shopping assistants
AI that helps a shopper choose the right product, not just answer support questions. Amazon's Alexa for Shopping and Salesforce Agentforce both take a plain-language question and return a specific product recommendation.
2. Real-time personalisation
Product pages, recommendations and offers that adjust to each shopper individually as they browse. Personalisation leaders see a 5–15% revenue lift and 10–30% marketing-efficiency gain (McKinsey).
3. Social commerce
Buying without leaving the app, directly from a post or a livestream. TikTok Shop alone reached $15.82B in sales in 2025, up 108% year-on-year.
4. Visual search & AR
Point a camera to find a product, or see it before buying it. Google Shopping now combines Google Lens visual search with a virtual try-on feature showing an item on an avatar.
5. Unified product content management
One central system holding every product's data, images and specs, syncing automatically and consistently to every site, marketplace and retailer. Akeneo and Salsify are the two most widely used platforms for this.
Sources: About Amazon, Salesforce, McKinsey, Search Engine Land, Google Shopping Blog, Akeneo, Salsify.
