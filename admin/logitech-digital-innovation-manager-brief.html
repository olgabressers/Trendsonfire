Essential facts
  • Founded 2 October 1981 in Apples, Switzerland. Dual headquarters: Lausanne, Switzerland and San Jose, California. Listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange (LOGN) and Nasdaq (LOGI).
  • CEO since 1 December 2023: Hanneke Faber, previously Group President at Unilever, with prior senior roles at Ahold Delhaize and Procter & Gamble.
  • Fiscal 2026 (year ended 31 March 2026) net sales reached $4.84B, up 6% in US dollars. Video Collaboration (+10%) and Tablet Accessories (+12%) grew fastest of any named category; Gaming remains the largest single line at $1.41B.
  • The role sits inside the Omnichannel Digital Innovation & Product team, led by Raffaele Masi, Director and Global Head of Product & Omnichannel Digital Innovation.
  • Logitech's own online store is estimated at $310–313M in 2025 (ecommerceDB estimate) against $4.84B in total net sales — direct-to-consumer is the growth lever this role is built to pull, not the channel that carries the business today.
  • Location: Utrecht, Netherlands. Hybrid work model.
Terms used on this page
DTC — Direct-to-Consumer: sales made through a brand's own website rather than a retailer.
eTail — online sales made through a third-party retailer's own website (Amazon, Best Buy) rather than the brand's own store.
UGC — User-Generated Content: reviews, photos and videos shoppers create themselves.
R&R — Ratings & Reviews: the star-rating and written-review system on a product page.
Guided selling — an interactive tool that asks a shopper a few questions and recommends the matching product.
Configurator — a tool that lets a shopper build or customise a product before buying it.
Omnichannel — coordinating the shopping experience across a brand's own site, physical retail and third-party retailers as one consistent journey.
AOV — Average Order Value: the average amount spent per completed order.
ROBO — Research Online, Buy Offline: a shopper researches a product online before buying it in a physical store.
FTE — Full-Time Equivalent: a standardised way of counting headcount.
KPI — Key Performance Indicator: a metric used to track performance against a target.
CAGR — Compound Annual Growth Rate: the average yearly growth rate over a multi-year period.

  1. The posting doesn't ask this role to invent guided selling and configuration from a blank page — Logitech already has a live Configurator and G HUB device-personalisation software in market. The mandate is to scale, connect and measure what already exists across more product lines and more markets.
  2. "Where to Buy" shows Logitech has already solved the data-visibility half of the direct-versus-retail tension named in Section 02 — the next step, and this role's remit, is closing the loop from campaign click to guided selling, ratings and reviews, and AI-enabled experience on its own product pages.

The Job Description
Global Digital Innovation Manager — full posting, by section
Job title: Global Digital Innovation Manager · Team: Omnichannel Digital Innovation & Product · Location: Utrecht, Netherlands · Work model: Hybrid · Employment: full-time.
Responsibilities
  1. Lead innovation projects and roadmap — from concept through post-launch execution, coordinating cross-functional teams and using data to optimise performance.
  2. Lead Ratings & Reviews / UGC enablement — maximise the capabilities of ratings-and-reviews and user-generated-content solutions across direct-to-consumer channels, manage vendor relationships and quarterly business reviews.
  3. Lead Guided Selling & Configurator initiatives — oversee interactive product finders and configuration experiences, manage vendor relationships and conversion insights.
  4. Enable the roadmap for AI-enabled experiences — contribute to the AI innovation roadmap and identify use cases that enhance customer engagement and streamline operations.
  5. Enable local eCommerce excellence execution — monitor local rollout across DTC and eTail channels, ensuring consistency across regional strategies.
  6. Elevate team capabilities — manage and mentor a team of digital innovation specialists, oversee vendor onboarding, budget allocation and negotiations, and lead training programmes.
Key Qualifications
  1. Able to inspire, motivate and influence teams while driving change and experimenting with new strategies.
  2. Demonstrated success building, scaling and leading geographically and culturally diverse teams, with a hands-on leadership approach.
  3. 5–8 years of experience in Digital, Product or eCommerce roles, ideally with global or regional scope.
  4. Storyteller with a knack for innovative thinking and data savviness.
  5. Multiple years developing a team of professionals, preferably in a matrixed or global environment.
  6. Strategic and critical thinking with a results-driven approach.
  7. Proficiency in budget management, vendor negotiations and project management.
  8. Deep knowledge of eCommerce trends, platforms and customer-experience optimisation.
  9. Strong understanding of omnichannel strategies and scalable online-programme execution.
  10. Experience creating global guidelines and best practices; experience with user-generated-content tools like Bazaarvoice is highly valued.
Source: official LinkedIn job posting, cross-checked verbatim against Logitech's own careers system (requisition 147529).
