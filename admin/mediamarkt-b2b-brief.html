Interview Brief — Head of B2B · Store - B2B, Benelux

MediaMarkt Benelux: B2B built from 7 stores to 20 hubs — still 12% of the business

€18.4B in group sales over nine months, adjusted EBIT up 22%, online at 28.2% of sales. Inside that, a Netherlands B2B unit — MediaMarkt Zakelijk — that launched in seven stores in 2019 and now runs 25 specialists across roughly 20 hubs. The role: one commercial B2B framework across hardware, software, services and payment, full P&L accountability, reporting to the Chief Commercial Officer.

Job posting on LinkedIn → Sources: Ceconomy Q3/9M FY2025/26 results (30 Jul 2026), Ceconomy FY2025 results, Wikipedia (Ceconomy, MediaMarkt), careers.mediamarktsaturn.com, emerce.nl, dutchitchannel.nl Updated 11 August 2026
Key conclusions
The company
Ceconomy AG — parent of MediaMarkt and Saturn. Nine-month FY2025/26 group sales of €18.4B (+5.0% adjusted), adjusted EBIT of €342M (+22% YoY) — the 14th consecutive quarter of profitable growth. Online sales at 28.2% of total (+190bps YoY). Over 1,000 stores across 11 European countries, 50,000+ employees.
The role
Head of B2B, Benelux — reporting to the Chief Commercial Officer. Drive the implementation of the international B2B strategy across the Benelux, build one commercial framework spanning hardware, software, services and payment, and take full P&L accountability for turnover and earnings before general expenses across the Netherlands B2B sales organisation — approximately 25 specialists distributed across roughly 20 hubs.
Programme context
B2B currently represents approximately 12% of total business, described in the role's own posting as carrying "significant untapped potential." The Netherlands B2B unit, MediaMarkt Zakelijk, launched in seven stores in May 2019 and has scaled to roughly 20 hubs.
Key opportunity
MediaMarkt Business runs the same commercial model in Spain, Italy, Belgium and Germany. Spain scaled from 10 to 87 stores within three years under that brand — a concrete precedent for what a structured, well-resourced B2B build-out inside MediaMarkt can look like once applied to the Benelux.

Role Analysis
3 structural challenges this role is built to solve
01
Thin coverage across 20 hubs
  • 25 B2B specialists distributed across roughly 20 hubs in the Netherlands — close to one specialist per hub for a nationwide SME sales motion
  • The role is explicitly asked to implement "structured performance management systems" — meaning consistent performance management is not yet standard across the network
  • The opportunity: move from thin, uneven hub coverage to a structured, performance-managed national footprint
Source: careers.mediamarktsaturn.com — official Head of B2B job posting
02
One B2B framework not yet built
  • The posting asks the role to "construct" comprehensive B2B solutions across hardware, software, services and payment — language that describes building a proposition, not managing an existing one
  • MediaMarkt Zakelijk launched with a hardware-led range (notebooks, displays, office equipment) in 2019; software, services and payment sit alongside it rather than inside one sellable framework
  • The opportunity: convert four separate product and service strands into one commercial B2B proposition with rapid rollout capability
Source: LinkedIn job posting · Emerce, 2019 launch coverage
03
Receivables & non-sales activity uncentralised
  • The posting names "addressing outstanding receivables" as a standing operational task, not a one-off clean-up
  • It also asks the role to prepare the organisation "for centralised non-sales activities" — meaning these currently sit outside a central operating model
  • The opportunity: bring receivables management and non-sales operations into one governed process ahead of national scale-up
Source: careers.mediamarktsaturn.com — official Head of B2B job posting

Opportunity Map
Commercial, operational, strategic & stakeholder opportunities — and how to address them
Commercial opportunities
B2B share stuck at ~12% of total business
The role's own posting names this "significant untapped potential" — the single largest commercial lever in the brief.
How to address it
Build the one B2B commercial framework the posting asks the role to "construct" — spanning hardware, software, services and payment — then apply it consistently across all ~20 hubs and the SME segment named explicitly in the posting.
KPI
B2B revenue as % of total MediaMarkt Benelux business, tracked against the ~12% baseline.
Hub network underused relative to demonstrated demand
MediaMarkt's own published customer references (Draftec, Go! Kinderopvang, Senioren Tab, Tutto Bello!, Zókora) and the Amsterdam ArenA hub's organic demand from large employers show the model converts — it isn't resourced at scale.
How to address it
Grow structured hub coverage beyond today's ~20 using the account-manager model already proven, prioritising sectors already converting — education, hospitality, healthcare, engineering, logistics.
KPI
Number of actively performing hubs and new named accounts acquired per quarter.
Financing lever (topi rental) likely underused as a sales tool
Rental financing lowers the SME purchase barrier but sits as one checkout option among several rather than a proactively sold feature.
How to address it
Train the 25 specialists to lead with financing options on larger SME deals rather than present rental as a passive checkout toggle.
KPI
% of B2B transaction value financed via rental vs. outright purchase.
Operational opportunities
Receivables management not centralised
The posting names "addressing outstanding receivables" as a standing task, not a one-off clean-up.
How to address it
Bring receivables into one governed, hub-wide process with clear ownership and escalation rules.
KPI
Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) across the B2B book.
Thin specialist coverage — close to 1:1 per hub
25 specialists across ~20 hubs leaves little redundancy or consistent performance management.
How to address it
Implement the "structured performance management systems" the posting explicitly calls for, benchmarked hub-to-hub.
KPI
Revenue and active accounts per specialist, benchmarked across hubs.
Non-sales activities not yet centralised
The posting asks the role to prepare the organisation "for centralised non-sales activities" — meaning they currently sit outside a central operating model.
How to address it
Design and roll out one centralised non-sales operating model — admin, fulfilment coordination, service scheduling — across all hubs.
KPI
% of non-sales processes running through the centralised model.
Strategic opportunities
International B2B strategy not yet localised to the Benelux
The posting's own first line is to "drive the implementation of international B2B strategy within the Benelux" — the strategy exists at group level but isn't yet built out locally.
How to address it
Translate the international B2B strategy into a Benelux-specific commercial framework with rapid rollout capability, as named in the posting.
KPI
Time from strategy definition to first-hub rollout; framework adoption rate across hubs.
Two structurally different Benelux B2B models
The Netherlands runs a physical-hub-plus-account-manager model; Belgium runs a self-service digital portal for employee benefits and gifting. No unified Benelux B2B model exists today.
How to address it
Build the bridge between the two — a shared commercial framework that works for both markets rather than exporting one model wholesale.
KPI
Belgium B2B growth and shared-framework adoption across both markets.
Stakeholder & organisational opportunities
Cross-border alignment with international B2B leadership
B2B is also owned at the international level — the COO's scope explicitly includes B2B across Spain, Benelux, Poland and Turkey — while the role itself reports to the Benelux CCO.
How to address it
Establish a regular alignment cadence with international B2B leadership and the CCO to keep the Benelux build-out consistent with group strategy.
KPI
Cadence of cross-border strategic reviews; time-to-decision on cross-market initiatives.
External network under-leveraged
The posting explicitly names external networking through business clubs and events as part of the role.
How to address it
Build a structured external-network programme that feeds SME lead generation, rather than ad hoc attendance.
KPI
Leads and partnerships sourced from external network activity per quarter.

B2B Network
B2B footprint & the Benelux precedent
20
B2B hubs in the Netherlands today
Grown from 7 stores at MediaMarkt Zakelijk's May 2019 launch (Amsterdam ArenA, Utrecht Hoog Catharijne, Den Haag, Rotterdam Centrum, Breda, Tilburg, Eindhoven Ekkersrijt) plus an online B2B channel. As of a 2022 trade-press count, 10 of the Netherlands' 50 MediaMarkt stores served the business market — the network has since grown to ~20 hubs per the current job posting.
32
Total MediaMarkt stores, Belgium
Belgium runs its own MediaMarkt Business model — not hub-based like the Netherlands, but a self-service B2B portal (portalb2b.mediamarkt.be) for employee reward programmes, home-office equipment and business gifting, billed on a consolidated invoice with deferred payment.
Sources: Dutch IT Channel, Emerce — 2019 NL launch coverage · Official job posting — current hub count · RetailDetail — Belgium store count · mediamarkt.be — MediaMarkt Business platform
The two Benelux markets run structurally different B2B models today: the Netherlands is a physical-hub-plus-account-manager model built for SME procurement; Belgium is a self-service digital portal built for employee benefits and gifting. Unifying the two — the "international B2B strategy within the Benelux" named in the role's own posting — is the structural task, not replicating a model from outside the region.

How "topi" rental works
A hardware-as-a-service financing layer MediaMarkt Business plugs into checkout — not a MediaMarkt product itself
#StepWhat happens
1Select at checkoutBusiness customer picks IT hardware (laptops, tablets, smartphones, accessories) from MediaMarkt Business and chooses "Rent with topi" instead of buying outright
2Register with topiA short, non-binding sign-up; topi — not MediaMarkt — becomes the financing party and legal owner of the device for the rental term
3Pick a term6, 12, 24 or 36 months, paid via monthly SEPA direct debit due in advance, booked as an operating expense rather than a capital purchase
4Receive & useMediaMarkt delivers as normal; the device carries the manufacturer's warranty, with optional "topi Care" add-ons covering accidental damage (up to 2 claims/year)
5End of termReturn the device, extend 6 months, purchase it at an agreed price, or upgrade to a new one
MediaMarkt is one of several retail partners on topi's platform (alongside Conrad and others) — topi supplies the financing and ownership layer, MediaMarkt supplies the product and delivery. Sources: topi.eu — Rent with topi · topi.eu — FAQs
