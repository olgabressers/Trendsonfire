Interview Brief — Service Director Benelux · HVAC Service Operations

Carrier Global: a €60M service P&L across Carrier, CIAT and Viessmann

Carrier's FY2025 group revenue fell 3% to $21.7B on a soft Residential and Light Commercial market. Its aftermarket service business did the opposite — a fifth consecutive year of double-digit growth. The Service Director Benelux role sits at that exact intersection: full P&L ownership of a €60M, 200+ person service organisation spanning Carrier, CIAT and Viessmann aftermarket, with a double-digit growth target through 2030, in a Dutch market where certified HVAC technicians are in short supply.

Sources: Carrier Global FY2025/Q1 2026 earnings releases (ir.carrier.com), SEC filings, Trane Technologies, Johnson Controls, Daikin Europe, Vaillant Group results, ABN AMRO & Mordor Intelligence NL market research, LinkedIn job posting (provided) Updated 14 July 2026
Key conclusions
The company
$21.7B FY2025 group revenue (-3% YoY). Climate Solutions Europe segment: $5.04B. Acquired Viessmann Climate Solutions for $14.2B in January 2024, adding a premier European residential/light-commercial HVAC and heat pump business to Carrier's own commercial climate portfolio.
The role
Service Director Benelux — full P&L ownership for Carrier, CIAT and Viessmann aftermarket service across the Netherlands and Belgium: maintenance, commissioning, repairs, modernization and parts. 200+ headcount, €60M revenue, double-digit annual growth targets through 2030. Based in Utrecht, ~40% regional travel.
Market context
The Dutch heat pump market is forecast to grow from $505M (2025) to $628M (2030). From 2026, homeowners must fit a hybrid heat pump or another sustainable alternative when a central heating system needs replacing — while nearly 2,000 consumer complaints were logged in 2024 over installer availability. Growth and a technician shortage are colliding in the exact market this role covers.
Honest fit assessment
Olga's background has no direct HVAC or field-service industry experience. What transfers directly: a €150M P&L and a 240+ person team across 26 European countries at Richemont, a Professional Doctorate in Engineering in Logistics & Supply Chain, and four years as a supply-chain and process consultant for industrial and FMCG clients. The genuine gap — HVAC/maintenance domain knowledge — is named explicitly in Section 03 and is the first item in the priority stack below.

Role Analysis
3 priorities this role is built around
01
Integrating three service brands into one P&L
  • Carrier completed the $14.2B Viessmann Climate Solutions acquisition in January 2024, adding Viessmann's European residential/light-commercial heat pump and boiler business to Carrier's own commercial portfolio and CIAT's
  • The role's scope — maintenance, commissioning, repairs, modernization and parts across all three brands — is the aftermarket expression of that integration
  • The task is turning three separate service organisations into one accountable, single-P&L operation
Source: Carrier 10-Q, Q1 2024 (Viessmann acquisition)
02
Growing headcount against a technician shortage
  • Nearly 2,000 Dutch consumer complaints were logged in 2024 over installer/technician availability, and wage inflation for certified technicians is pushing up system costs
  • From 2026, homeowners must fit a hybrid heat pump or comparable sustainable alternative when replacing a central heating system — a policy shift expected to raise installation demand through 2025–2026
  • The job ad names hiring, retention and succession planning explicitly — this labor market is why
Source: ABN AMRO research
03
Converting connected devices into aftermarket growth
  • Connected devices in the field were up more than 25% in Q1 2026, and Carrier expects a sixth consecutive year of double-digit aftermarket growth group-wide
  • Commercial HVAC orders, including data-center wins, were a major growth driver into 2026
  • Digital transformation and connected services are named directly in the job ad as a core accountability for this role
Source: Carrier Q1 2026 earnings call transcript
