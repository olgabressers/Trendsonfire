Interview Brief — Business Director · Client Portfolio

Monks: EMEA net revenue down 20.3%, group margin up 600 basis points, and a client portfolio to grow

Monks, a digital advertising and marketing agency owned by S4Capital plc, reported H1 2026 net revenue of £308.0 million (4.7% down like-for-like) with operational EBITDA up 82.7% to £38.0 million. EMEA fell 20.3% like-for-like while the group positions AI tools at the centre of new business. The role: executive growth driver and strategic lead for one of its highest-profile client portfolios in the Dutch-language market.

Sources: S4Capital interim results 2026 and audited results 2025, MediaNews4U, monks.com, LinkedIn job postingUpdated 19 October 2026
Key challenges — why the business is shrinking and what to offer clients to recover
Why revenue is shrinking
  • Technology clients moved budget to AI infrastructure (41% of revenue).
  • Technology Services lost clients: down 29.9% in FY2025.
  • BMW cut scope, mainly in EMEA: EMEA down 20.3% in H1 2026.
  • Longer sales cycles and cautious clients.
What to offer clients to recover
  • AI production on Monks.Flow: faster, cheaper content (BMW MINIWOOD, Google Gemini).
  • Output-based and subscription pricing instead of time-based.
  • Measurement that proves return (Euro Car Parts, Schuh).
Sources: S4Capital, Audited 2025 Results (24 March 2026) · S4Capital, Interim Results for 2026 (5 August 2026) · monks.com/work

Why net revenue went down
Reasons the group itself gives
  • Technology clients cut marketing to fund AI infrastructure. Technology clients were 41% of FY2025 revenue (2024: 45%); the group says they allocated more spend to building AI infrastructure. H1 2026 cites reduced technology and hyperscaler marketing spending.
  • Client losses in Technology Services. Technology Services net revenue fell 29.9% like-for-like in FY2025 and 7.4% in H1 2026.
  • BMW scope reduction, primarily in EMEA. EMEA net revenue fell 20.3% like-for-like in H1 2026.
  • Longer sales cycles. Cited for H1 2026, particularly in Technology Services.
  • Client caution. Cited as the general driver of the FY2025 decline of 8.4% like-for-like.
Sources: S4Capital, Audited 2025 Results (24 March 2026) · S4Capital, Interim Results for 2026 (5 August 2026)

Role Analysis
3 structural challenges this role is built to solve
01
Europe, Middle East and Africa is the fastest-shrinking region
  • FY2025 EMEA net revenue fell 19.0% to £99.9 million (19.6% like-for-like); H1 2026 fell a further 18.9% to £42.0 million (20.3% like-for-like)
  • The Americas, 81% of H1 2026 net revenue, fell 0.8% like-for-like in the same period
  • The group attributes the EMEA decline to a scope reduction at BMW, primarily in EMEA
Source: S4Capital, Audited 2025 Results (24 March 2026) · S4Capital, Interim Results for 2026 (5 August 2026)
02
Technology clients are redirecting spend to AI infrastructure
  • The group attributes its FY2025 decline to client caution, especially among technology clients allocating spend to building AI infrastructure, and to client losses in Technology Services
  • Technology clients were 41% of FY2025 revenue (2024: 45%); Technology Services net revenue fell 29.9% like-for-like
  • Monks.Flow won AI-based Marketing Solution of the Year at the AI Breakthrough awards, and the group states its proprietary AI solutions are at the heart of all new business efforts; commercial production is moving from time-based to output-based pricing, including subscription models
Source: S4Capital, Audited 2025 Results (24 March 2026) · S4Capital, Interim Results for 2026 (5 August 2026)
03
A smaller team and a new EMEA leadership layer
  • Headcount fell from 6,879 to 6,156 Monks in twelve months (10.5% down); H1 2026 margin gain is attributed to cost actions, fewer non-billable roles and back-office efficiencies
  • Nirvik Singh becomes CEO of EMEA Marketing Services on 1 October 2026, overseeing brand strategy, creative craft, content orchestration and data-driven media
  • Group guidance for 2026: like-for-like net revenue down mid-single digits, operational EBITDA of £85 million, margin target of around 20% over the longer term
Source: S4Capital, Interim Results for 2026 (5 August 2026) · MediaNews4U, 22 September 2026
