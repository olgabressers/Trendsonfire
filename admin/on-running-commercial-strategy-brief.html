Interview Brief — Head of Commercial Strategy · Global Commercial

On Holding: CHF 3 billion, new Co-CEOs, and a Global Commercial portfolio to architect

On Holding crossed CHF 3.0 billion in net sales in 2025 (+30.0%), then decelerated to +13.5% in Q2 2026 the same quarter founders David Allemann and Caspar Coppetti returned as Co-CEOs alongside a new Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer. The role: architect On's omnichannel commercial strategy across DTC, wholesale and own-retail channels, and own the Global Commercial project portfolio — including "New Sports," the priority named directly in the posting.

Pricing discipline — no discounts
A no-markdown policy held since On's 2010 founding

Role Analysis
3 structural challenges this role is built to solve
01
Growth decelerating after a record year
  • FY2025 net sales grew 30.0% reported (35.6% constant currency) to CHF 3,014.0 million — On's first year above CHF 3 billion
  • Q2 2026 net sales grew 13.5% reported (21.6% constant currency) to CHF 850.3 million — below analyst estimates of roughly CHF 878 million, and shares fell sharply on the day of the release
  • Full-year 2026 guidance was narrowed at the Q2 release to the lower end of the range set in March 2026
Source: On, FY2025 and Q2 2026 results releases; Yahoo Finance and SGB Media on the share-price reaction
02
A founder-led reset landing in the final year of the strategy
  • Co-founders David Allemann and Caspar Coppetti became Co-CEOs on 1 May 2026, succeeding Martin Hoffmann — CEO for 5 years and CFO for 13 years before that
  • Frank Sluis joined as Chief Financial Officer the same day, from Ahold Delhaize; Scott Maguire's mandate expanded to President and Chief Operating Officer
  • The change lands inside the final year of the three-year strategy set at On's October 2023 Investor Day, six days before the next Investor Day on 22 September 2026
Source: swissinfo.ch · sgbonline.com · On, Investor Day announcement
03
A wholesale-majority business scaling a DTC ambition
  • Wholesale still carried 58.2% of FY2025 net sales (CHF 1,753.4 million) even as DTC grew faster (+33.7% reported vs. +27.5% for wholesale)
  • DTC reached a record 45.7% of net sales in Q2 2026 — the channel this role is asked to architect alongside wholesale and nearly 70 owned retail locations
  • The posting names deep understanding of "digital, wholesale, and own-retail channels" as a required qualification
Source: On, FY2025 and Q2 2026 results releases; culture.on.com job posting

Omni-channel strategy proposal — three sequenced projects
01
Channel Role Architecture
Formalize what each channel is for: DTC carries premieres, the full line, brand experience and consumer data; wholesale carries reach, access and trust through specialist running retailers; owned retail is the category showcase and the home for New Sports. Output: a model × channel × market segmentation matrix and escalation rules for channel conflict.
Segmentation matrix Conflict escalation rules
Why firstThe foundation the other two projects depend on.
02
Apparel Attach — Converting Footwear Accounts Into Apparel
Take 15–20 key accounts where On is already strong in footwear and build apparel from zero to a meaningful share. Mechanics: mandatory assortment breadth in pre-order, a branded head-to-toe zone in place of a wall of sneakers, sales-associate training, and weekly sell-out tracking.
Apparel attach rate Weekly sell-out tracking
ResultThe fastest path to revenue growth — apparel is under 10% of sales against an existing footwear customer base.
03
New Market Entry Playbook — Asia & the Middle East
A repeatable playbook, not a one-off launch: partner-selection criteria, starting assortment, trade terms, an 18-month plan, and a defined trigger point for moving from distributor to owned presence. Pilot in one market, then replicate.
Partner-selection criteria 18-month market plan
FitAnswers "market expansion," named directly in the job posting.
04
Wholesale Sell-Through Transparency
Weekly sell-out data from key wholesale accounts, not just sell-in orders, as the basis for allocation, replenishment and joint business plan reviews.
Sell-out vs. sell-in JBP review cadence
WhyVisibility into what actually sells, not just what ships.

Uptake of new commercial capabilities across markets after rollout.
BenchmarkProsci's change management research: 88% of organisations with excellent change management practices met or exceeded their objectives.
Change adoption timeline
Speed of smooth transition and integration within the markets the posting names as the standard.
BenchmarkProsci: initiatives with highly effective executive sponsorship are 79% likely to meet objectives, versus 27% with ineffective sponsorship — sponsorship strength is the direct driver of adoption speed.
Capability sustainability
Whether built capabilities are still in active use — not just launched — at 90 and 180 days.
BenchmarkOn's own apparel and accessories share grew from 5.1% to 7.0% of net sales in one year (+190 basis points) — the sustained-adoption pace of a new category taking hold, rather than a one-quarter spike.
Best-practice framework compliance
Consistency of project management standards applied across the portfolio, named directly in the posting.
BenchmarkProsci: 59% of organisations using a structured change methodology achieved good-or-excellent change management effectiveness, versus lower, inconsistent results without one.
Stakeholder & governance
Cross-functional decision velocity
Speed of decisions across a matrixed organisation spanning regions and functions.
BenchmarkOn's own leadership reset: the Co-CEOs, Chief Financial Officer and expanded President/Chief Operating Officer mandate all took effect on a single date, 1 May 2026 — the standard of coordinated decision-making this role's stakeholder map now has to match.
Regional stakeholder alignment
A named accountability in the posting's own mission statement.
Benchmark3 regions to keep aligned — EMEA, Americas, Asia-Pacific — while Asia-Pacific scales fastest (+96.4% FY2025) and Americas slowest (+17.6%).
Reporting cadence to the Co-CEOs & Chief Financial Officer
Standing rhythm through a newly formed leadership team.
BenchmarkOn's own public reporting rhythm — quarterly results plus an annual Investor Day (next one 22 September 2026) — the cadence this role's internal reporting should feed and match.
Central point-of-contact effectiveness
Resolving interdependencies across teams — named directly in the posting.
Benchmark9 standing relationships to hold at once — 3 Project Management Offices (Operations, Technology, Finance) across 3 regions (EMEA, Americas, Asia-Pacific) — the full span the posting's "central point of contact" language implies.
