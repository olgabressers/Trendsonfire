Interview Brief — Head of Commercial Strategy · Global Commercial

On Holding: CHF 3 billion, new Co-CEOs, and a Global Commercial portfolio to architect

On Holding AG (NYSE: ONON) crossed CHF 3.0 billion in net sales for the first time in 2025 — up 30.0% reported, 35.6% at constant currency — then grew 13.5% reported (21.6% constant currency) in the second quarter of 2026, a deceleration the market read sharply: shares fell after the release, with net sales landing below analyst estimates of roughly CHF 878 million. The same period, co-founders David Allemann and Caspar Coppetti returned as Co-CEOs, Frank Sluis joined as Chief Financial Officer, and Scott Maguire's mandate expanded to President and Chief Operating Officer — a reset landing in the final year of the three-year strategy set at On's October 2023 Investor Day, six days before the next one, on 22 September 2026. The role: architect the omnichannel commercial strategy across On's Direct-to-Consumer ("DTC"), wholesale and own-retail channels, own the Global Commercial project portfolio, and lead change management for new commercial capabilities — including "New Sports," the priority named directly in the posting.

Sources: press.on-running.com, SEC Form 20-F & 6-K filings, culture.on.com job posting, Deckers Brands, adidas AG, Amer Sports, Nike, Berkshire Hathaway, swissinfo.ch, sgbonline.com Updated 16 September 2026
Key conclusions
The company
Swiss premium sportswear brand founded in Zurich in 2010. Crossed CHF 3.0 billion in net sales for the first time in 2025 (+30.0% reported, +35.6% constant currency). Direct-to-Consumer ("DTC") sales grew faster than wholesale, and Asia-Pacific ("APAC") grew 96.4%. Roger Federer became an investor and product partner ahead of the September 2021 IPO on the NYSE.
The role
Head of Commercial Strategy, based in Zurich, leading the Commercial Programs & Project Management team inside Global Strategy. Architect On's omnichannel commercial strategy across DTC, wholesale and own-retail channels; own the Global Commercial project portfolio; lead commercial programs spanning sales elevation, market expansion and omnichannel development; and act as the central point of contact across Operations, Technology and Finance stakeholders.
Company context
Growth decelerated to 13.5% reported (21.6% constant currency) in the second quarter of 2026, missing analyst estimates and sending shares down sharply on the day of the release. The same period brought a full leadership reset: co-founders David Allemann and Caspar Coppetti became Co-CEOs, Frank Sluis joined as Chief Financial Officer, and Scott Maguire's mandate expanded to President and Chief Operating Officer — landing in the final year of the three-year strategy set at On's October 2023 Investor Day, with the next Investor Day six days after this brief's last update.
Key opportunity
The posting names "New Sports" as a strategic priority for this role. On has expanded into tennis (Iga Świątek, Ben Shelton, João Fonseca, and "THE ROGER" shoe line with Federer) and, since January 2026, padel (World No.1 Arturo Coello) — the opening is to build the omnichannel commercial framework for these categories before they scale, at the same moment On's own leadership and three-year strategy are both being reset.
