Interview Brief — Algemeen Directeur (Managing Director) · Rotterdam

Remastered: an immersive digital art experience beneath the Erasmusbrug

A 1,500 m² venue in Rotterdam where the Dutch Old Masters — Bosch, Vermeer, Rembrandt, Van Gogh, Mondrian — are reinterpreted at monumental scale across Europe's largest indoor LED screen, with an original score by DJ/producer Sam Feldt.

Updated 16 August 2026
Remastered's Van Gogh "Starry Night" room, Rotterdam · remastered.nl
Key conclusions
The company
Remastered, an immersive digital art experience beneath the Erasmusbrug, Rotterdam. Opened 30 June 2021, founded by Robin Groeneveld and Dick van Zuylen (former Mojo Events director). 1,500 m² venue, 60 projectors, Europe's largest indoor LED screen. Roughly 500,000 visitors at its five-year mark (mid-2026); the job posting itself cites "450,000+".
The role
Algemeen Directeur (Managing Director) — the first dedicated commercial leadership hire. Grow toward 100,000–125,000 annual visitors, build corporate events, product launches and business arrangements, create sponsorship, partnership and hospitality revenue, improve visitor experience and dwell time, professionalize operations, and prepare the concept for international expansion. Creative direction and content stay with the founders.
Market context
Dutch day attractions logged 46M+ visits in 2025, growth stabilizing at +0.4% YoY. Europe's immersive entertainment market is valued at $36.4B in 2025, projected to reach $132.3B by 2031. Rotterdam's own tourism base grew 2.4% in 2025, with business/MICE tourism up 25%.
Key opportunity
Remastered has proven organic visitor demand at scale on word-of-mouth alone. The opportunity: build a multi-line commercial model — corporate, sponsorship, hospitality — and the operating model a second location can be built on.

Visitor mix — tourist vs. local, where operators actually disclose it

Of the 12 experiences above, only 4 publish anything about visitor origin, and none — Remastered included — publish a revenue split by visitor type (tourist / local / business). That level of segment reporting does not appear to exist publicly anywhere in this category. Building it would be a new capability, not something benchmarked against an established industry standard.
