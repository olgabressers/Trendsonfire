Remastered: current P&L, modeled from public data

Remastered does not publish a P&L. This model builds one from the only public inputs that exist: confirmed visitor milestones, published ticket prices, and industry-benchmark margins for the Hotels, Tourism & Amusement sector. Every figure below is marked as either sourced fact or a stated assumption.

Updated 19 August 2026

What's fact vs. what's modeled

Sourced fact
  • Ticket price: €24.50 adult / €16.50 child — remastered.nl FAQ
  • 200,000th visitor milestone, June 2023 — Pretwerk
  • ~500,000 visitors at the 5-year mark, July 2026 — AD.nl via Drimble
  • Industry EBITDA margin 13.83%, Net margin 4.12% — Hotels, Tourism & Amusement industry, trailing twelve months to Q2 2026, CSIMarket
  • 100,000–125,000 annual visitor target — LinkedIn job posting
Modeled assumption
  • Current annual visitor pace: ~97,000/yr — calculated as (500,000 − 200,000) ÷ 37 months × 12, the only interval with two dated milestones. No confirmed last-12-months figure is published.
  • Visitor mix: 85% adult / 15% child — back-solved to reproduce the €23.30 blended ticket price used below; not disclosed by Remastered.
  • Cost structure: modeled on industry-benchmark margins, not Remastered's own figures — Remastered publishes no P&L or cost breakdown.
  • Excludes group bookings, corporate/venue-hire and sponsorship revenue — no disclosed volume or frequency exists to quantify these lines.
