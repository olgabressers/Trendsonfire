Remastered: current P&L, modeled from public data

Remastered does not publish a P&L. Profit and loss — the terms used on this page

Revenue net of value-added tax — ticket sales after the 9% Dutch attraction rate is removed. The published €23.30 ticket is €21.38 of revenue.
Fixed costs — people, marketing, technology, property, depreciation. €1.83M a year, unchanged whether 80,000 or 125,000 people visit.
Variable costs — reseller commission and ticketing fees, 8.2% of revenue. They rise only when a ticket sells.
Contribution per visitor — €19.63. What one extra visitor adds once variable cost is taken off.
Break-even — fixed costs ÷ contribution per visitor = 93,388 visitors. Below it the year is a loss.
Result before tax — revenue less all costs, before the tax authority takes its share.
Corporate income tax — 19% to €200,000 of profit, 25.8% above, at Dutch 2026 rates.
Net profit after tax — what is left for the owner and the investor.
Depreciation — the build-out and the experience upgrade spread across the years they are used, rather than charged in one.
Capacity utilisation — visitors as a share of seats the venue could sell. 28.6% today.

Modeled assumption
  • Current annual visitor pace: 97,000/yr — calculated as (500,000 − 200,000) ÷ 37 months × 12, the only interval with two dated milestones. No confirmed last-12-months figure is published.
  • Visitor mix: 85% adult / 15% child — back-solved to reproduce the €23.30 blended ticket price used below; not disclosed by Remastered.
  • Cost structure: two independent models below — a bottom-up build by role and cost line, and a cross-check using industry-benchmark margins. Neither is Remastered's own figures; Remastered publishes no P&L or cost breakdown.
  • Dutch employer charges: 30% on top of gross salary, covering statutory social security (AWf, Aof, Zvw, Whk) and the statutory 8% holiday allowance.
  • Excludes group bookings and sponsorship revenue — no disclosed volume or frequency exists to quantify these lines. Corporate/venue-hire revenue is modeled separately below as a distinct opportunity, not folded into the ticketing P&L.
