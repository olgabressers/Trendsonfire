Interview Brief — Director KYC Center · Compliance Team, Legal Advisory

Team EIFFEL: €323M group, but the segment this role sits in just took a €5.1M writedown

Founded 1992, 2,936 FTE, 800+ clients, majority-owned by TowerBrook Capital Partners and Gilde Equity Management. The Financial Services & Compliance business line — home of the KYC Center — declined to €35.2M revenue in 2025 and absorbed a goodwill impairment, and is being folded into Legal Advisory from 2026.

Essential facts
  • Dutch interim/consultancy/project-management group, founded 1992 by Ferdi van Dommelen. Holding entity Equipe Holdings 3 B.V., HQ Marathon 4, Hilversum.
  • CEO since 9 Feb 2026: Jo Maes (ex-CEO of Ordina and Sopra Steria), replacing Gert-Jan Meppelink. CFO: Yke Bonenberg.
  • 2025 was a down year group-wide: revenue roughly flat, EBITDA fell 20-21%, net loss €38.4M, driven by lower billability and a cautious Dutch market.
  • The Financial Services & Compliance segment — where the KYC Center sits — was the worst-performing of the four: revenue down, EBITDA thinnest of any segment (6.1% margin), and a €5.1M goodwill impairment booked in 2025 due to "continued challenging market conditions" and pricing pressure.
  • From 2026, Team EIFFEL restructures from 4 business lines to 3, folding Financial Services into Legal Advisory and Finance Advisory — this Director role is being asked to build a "future-proof" unit inside a segment the company itself just wrote down and is dissolving as a standalone line.

The Job Description
Director, KYC Center — full posting, by section
Job title: Director KYC Center · Location: Hilversum · Function area: Legal, Finance & Business Development · Employment: full-time.
Responsibilities
  1. Develop and execute the KYC Center's growth strategy — own its commercial, operational and financial results.
  2. Build a future-proof KYC Center by integrating AI, data and technological innovation into distinctive service offerings.
  3. Build your own team of KYC specialists and foster a strong, high-performing culture.
  4. Generate new business, lead strategic client engagements, and build durable client relationships.
  5. Translate market developments, technological innovation, data and AI into scalable compliance solutions.
  6. Steer performance management, workforce planning, client satisfaction and continuous improvement of the Center.
Requirements
  1. University degree — e.g. Business Administration, Economics, Law or Finance.
  2. Minimum 10 years' experience in consulting, financial services, business development or a comparable commercial leadership role.
  3. Demonstrable experience building and scaling teams, propositions and business units.
  4. Experience in strategic sales, complex stakeholder management and executive-level decision-making.
  5. Deep knowledge of KYC, AML, compliance, risk or financial-crime-prevention topics.
Source: official LinkedIn job posting.

KPIs
KPI framework — four layers
Commercial & growth
KYC Center revenue growth
Growth against the segment's 2025 base of €35.2M, reversing the year-on-year decline.
New business & framework-contract share
New client wins and utilisation of framework agreements — the group runs 50% of revenue through framework contracts.
Win rate on strategic pursuits
% of qualified client opportunities converted to signed engagements, tracked from the strategic-sales mandate in the posting.
Cross-sell into adjacent lines
Clients buying KYC/CDD alongside other Financial Services & Compliance or Legal Advisory work, post-2026 restructuring.
Team & delivery
KYC specialist headcount & billability
Size and utilisation of the team built inside the Center — billability was the segment's stated 2025 weak point.
1st-year churn
Group-wide priority named in the 2025 Annual Report; total workforce turnover across Team EIFFEL ran at 41.6% in 2025.
Direct/indirect staffing ratio
Specialist-to-support-staff efficiency, benchmarked against the group's 2025 average of 6.0.
Specialist productivity
Cases closed and revenue generated per KYC specialist — the direct output of the team the posting asks this role to build.
Technology & innovation
AI/data tooling adoption
Rollout of AI-enabled applications inside KYC case work — the Group appointed a Head of AI and shipped its first applications in H2 2025.
Case cycle time
Time-to-close per KYC/CDD case as automation and data tooling are embedded into the workflow.
Automation coverage
% of case volume triaged or pre-populated by automated tooling before analyst review.
Rework rate
% of cases requiring rework from incomplete or inconsistent source data — a direct data-quality signal.
Financial & governance
Segment EBITDA margin
Recovery from Financial Services & Compliance's 2025 margin of 6.1% — the thinnest of the group's four segments.
Client satisfaction (NPS)
Measured annually group-wide; Team EIFFEL's 2025 customer satisfaction score was 7.9.
Goodwill impairment reversal signal
Whether FY2026 cash-flow performance closes the gap that triggered the 2025 €5.1M writedown.
Audit / finding closure rate
Compliance findings and reviews closed within SLA — the standard governance KPI for a compliance-as-a-service unit.
