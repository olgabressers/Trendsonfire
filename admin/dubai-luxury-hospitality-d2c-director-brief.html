Interview Brief — Director, Direct-to-Consumer · Reporting to the Chief Commercial Officer · Dubai

Dubai luxury travel: 19.59M visitors in 2025, 31% fewer passengers in the first half of 2026

Dubai closed 2025 with a third consecutive record year: 19.59 million international overnight visitors, 80.7% hotel occupancy and Dubai Duty Free sales of AED 8.68 billion. The regional conflict since the end of February 2026 cut Dubai International traffic by 31.3% in the first half and hotel revenue per available room by 31%. Recovery has been visible since April. The direct-to-consumer channel is being built into this market from the ground up.

Sources: Dubai Media Office / Department of Economy and Tourism, Dubai Airports, JLL via The National, Dubai Duty Free, SiteMinder, Mailchimp Updated 29 September 2026
Key conclusions
The role
Director of direct-to-consumer for a luxury hospitality and travel group, reporting to the Chief Commercial Officer. Owns profitable direct revenue across ecommerce, customer relationship management, data, acquisition, retention and customer experience, with a lean team: a Growth Manager, a Data Scientist, development capability and external campaign support.
The market
In 2025 Western Europe was Dubai's largest source market (21% of visitors), followed by the Gulf Cooperation Council, Commonwealth of Independent States and Eastern Europe, and South Asia at 15% each. Average daily rate rose 8% to AED 579.
2026
Occupancy fell to 36% in March and was back to 66% in August, 89% of August 2025. Booking windows have shortened, European visitors are down and Asian visitors up.
What it means for direct
With demand arriving later and from different countries, the owned guest database and direct channel are the parts of distribution the group can steer itself. A direct hotel booking averaged US$519 in 2024, over 60% more than an online travel agency booking.

What changed for a direct channel
Three shifts the direct-to-consumer build has to work with
01
Shorter booking windows
  • International guests are booking closer to arrival
  • Owned guest data and customer relationship management reach a guest in that shorter window without paying a third party per booking
Source: The National, 14 Sep 2026
02
A different source-market mix
  • European visitors down, Asian visitors up in 2026
  • Western Europe was the largest market in 2025 at 21%, so acquisition spend and content by market need rebalancing
Source: The National · Dubai Media Office
03
Margin per booking
  • Hotel websites averaged US$519 per booking in 2024, against US$320 via online travel agencies
  • With revenue per available room down 31%, each booking moved to direct keeps more of a smaller revenue pool
Source: SiteMinder Hotel Booking Trends, 125M reservations
