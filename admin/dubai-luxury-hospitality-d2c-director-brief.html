Soneva · Interview Brief — Director, Direct-to-Consumer · Reporting to the Chief Commercial Officer · Dubai

Soneva, Dubai luxury travel: 6.97M visitors January–August 2026, against 12.54M in 2025

International overnight visitors to Dubai fell 44% in the first eight months of 2026, and occupied room nights fell 26%, after the regional conflict that began at the end of February. Dubai International handled 31.3% fewer passengers in the first half. August was the strongest month since February: 869,000 visitors and 66% hotel occupancy, 89% of the August 2025 level. The direct-to-consumer channel is being built into this market from the ground up.

Sources: Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism, Dubai Airports, JLL via The National, Gulf News, Dubai Duty Free, SiteMinder, Mailchimp Updated 29 September 2026
Key conclusions
The role
Director of direct-to-consumer for a luxury hospitality and travel group, reporting to the Chief Commercial Officer. Owns profitable direct revenue across ecommerce, customer relationship management, data, acquisition, retention and customer experience, with a lean team: a Growth Manager, a Data Scientist, development capability and external campaign support.
2026 against 2025
January–August visitors 6.97M against 12.54M (−44%). Occupied room nights 21.61M against 29.03M (−26%). Room nights fell less than visitors, so the guests who came stayed longer.
Recovery
Occupancy went from 36% in March to 66% in August. South Asia and the Gulf Cooperation Council gained share of visitors in 2026; Western Europe and the Commonwealth of Independent States lost share. Booking windows have shortened.
What it means for direct
With demand arriving later and from different countries, the owned guest database and direct channel are the parts of distribution the group can steer itself. A direct hotel booking averaged US$516 in 2025, against US$312 through online travel agencies.

What changed for a direct channel
Three shifts the direct-to-consumer build has to work with
01
Shorter booking windows
  • International guests are booking closer to arrival
  • Owned guest data and customer relationship management reach a guest in that shorter window without paying a third party per booking
Source: The National, 14 Sep 2026
02
A different source-market mix
  • South Asia rose from 15% to 17% of visitors and the Gulf Cooperation Council from 15% to 16%
  • Western Europe fell from 21% to 20% and the Commonwealth of Independent States from 15% to 14%, so acquisition spend and content by market need rebalancing
Source: BW Travel · Dubai Media Office
03
Margin per booking
  • Hotel websites averaged US$516 per booking in 2025, against US$312 via online travel agencies; 2026 values not yet published
  • Dubai revenue per available room was AED 413 / €99 in January–August 2025 and fell 31% in January–June 2026; each booking moved to direct keeps more of that smaller pool
Source: SiteMinder Hotel Booking Trends, 130M+ reservations
