Chief Operating Officer Brief · The Padellers

The Padellers: from 1 court to 33 clubs in five years, with 17 more toward the stated 50+ target

Amsterdam-founded padel operator, one of the two largest commercial chains in the Netherlands. Founded April 2021, backed by DM Equity Partners since April 2022, now 33 club locations across the Netherlands and Germany with a stated ambition to reach 50+ and West European market leadership. The Chief Operating Officer role owns daily operations across every location, sitting directly alongside the founding team.

Sources: LinkedIn job posting, thepadellers.nl, LinkedIn company page, Adyen, dealmaker.nl, EY Netherlands, Basic-Fit FY2025 results Updated 7 September 2026
The Padellers · thepadellers.nl
Key conclusions
The company
Founded April 2021 by four co-founders. 33 club locations (Netherlands + Germany), 124 employees. Second-largest commercial padel chain in the Netherlands after Peakz Padel. Minority growth investment from DM Equity Partners since April 2022.
Chief Operating Officer — mandate
Six areas from the role posting: operational processes across multiple locations, translating strategy into execution, team leadership, quality & customer satisfaction, expansion & professionalisation, and bridging internal teams with external partners.
Scaling to 50+
The stated target is 50+ locations — 17 more clubs than today, across two countries. National court demand in the Netherlands is already growing faster than court supply.
Competitive position
Three commercial chains lead the Dutch market: Peakz Padel (first mover, largest), The Padellers (second, fastest-growing, only chain with a live German footprint), Plaza Padel (smaller, hospitality-led positioning).

Market context
Three trends shaping Dutch padel in 2026
  • 1

    Capacity, not demand, is the binding constraint

    876,000 people played padel in the Netherlands in 2025. Providers and locations grew 15% year-on-year, sector jobs grew 25% — but EY's own framing is that player growth is outpacing court growth, with the sharpest strain in wealthier, denser provinces like North Holland, The Padellers' home market.

    876,000 players, 2025 +15% providers vs +25% sector jobs
  • 2

    The market is consolidating around three commercial chains

    Peakz Padel, The Padellers and Plaza Padel have separated from a long tail of single-site and club-association operators. The Padellers is the fastest-growing of the three, and the only one already operating in a second country.

    3 commercial chains lead the market
  • 3

    New builds are getting bigger, not just more numerous

    Nationally, courts grew 25% against a 15% rise in locations in 2025 — new sites are averaging more courts than existing ones. Commercial operators already run 5.7 courts per location on average versus 4.5 for club associations. The Padellers' own German entry (Frankfurt, 9 courts) fits this bigger-build pattern.

    Courts +25% vs locations +15%, 2025 5.7 courts/commercial location average
Sources: EY Netherlands, March 2026 · AllesPadel, 2026 · allesoverpadel.nl.

KPIs
KPI framework — by function
Court economics & new-site ramp
Court utilisation rate
Booked hours ÷ available hours per court — the core throughput metric across 33 locations.
Benchmark 50%+ in Year 1, climbing to 60–70% by Year 3 for a well-run padel centre.
Investment payback period
Time to recover capex on a new club build.
Benchmark 2–4 years typical, faster when reusing existing buildings and utilisation exceeds 60%.
Break-even court count
Minimum court count for a new site to cover fixed costs.
Benchmark Approximately 6 courts per site in a medium-sized city at 55–65% utilisation.
Net margin
Bottom-line margin per club once operating at a mature run-rate.
Benchmark 10–25% net margin when well run.
Revenue mix & cost control
Ancillary revenue share
Non-court revenue as a share of total — coaching, events, retail, food & beverage, sponsorship.
Benchmark 20–35% of total revenue in a diversified padel centre.
Break-even occupancy rate
Minimum utilisation needed to cover fixed and variable costs.
Benchmark Target below 40% of available court hours.
Staff cost-to-revenue ratio
Total wage cost ÷ total revenue — the standard labour-efficiency check for a service-led operator.
Benchmark Target below 40%.
Monthly operating cost per site
Staff, maintenance, utilities, marketing and admin combined for one medium-sized club.
Benchmark Approximately $29,000/month for a medium facility.
Membership & customer retention
Membership retention rate
PINK loyalty and recurring-play members retained year-on-year.
Benchmark Padel-operator target 80%+ annually.
Fitness-industry average retention
Broader leisure/fitness comparator — a lower bar padel's social, group format should beat.
Benchmark 66.4% annual retention industry-wide, per the Health & Fitness Association's 2025 Benchmarking Report.
New-member churn window
Share of new members lost in their first months — the highest-leverage retention period.
Benchmark Close to half of new fitness/leisure members quit within six months industry-wide.
Group-format retention advantage
Padel is inherently a doubles/group sport — directly relevant to this comparator.
Benchmark Group or community-led formats show 56% lower cancellation than gym-only, solo memberships.
Team & people operations
Annual staff turnover (NL horeca/leisure)
Sector-wide comparator for a 124-person, multi-site hospitality-adjacent operator.
Benchmark Approximately 40% per year in Dutch horeca, more than double healthcare's 15.7%.
Cost of staff turnover
Recruitment, onboarding and lost-productivity cost of replacing staff.
Benchmark An estimated €1.4 billion per year sector-wide in Dutch horeca — about 6% of total sector revenue.
Staff cost-to-revenue ratio
Same efficiency metric as above, tracked at the people-management level.
Benchmark Target below 40% for a padel-centre operator.
Rent + staff share of operating costs
The two largest fixed-cost lines for any multi-site leisure facility business.
Benchmark Roughly 70% of total operating costs combined, across fitness facility operators.
